FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about your data security or app features? We've got answers.

Is my financial data uploaded to any cloud server? +
No. The application is completely offline-first. All databases and receipt files remain sandboxed in your device's local memory via SQLite.
How does the voice recognition work? +
The app utilizes your device's native speech recognition framework to convert spoken words into text locally or through OS-level voice services, without saving or relaying audio recordings to external databases.
Can I export my data for tax filing or backups? +
Yes, you can generate comprehensive PDF reports and summaries directly inside the app, and share them via standard mobile sharing channels (email, AirDrop, messaging, or files).

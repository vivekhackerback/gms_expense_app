Privacy Policy

Application: Expense Tracker (com.tplpro.expenseapp)

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Overview: Expense Tracker is designed with privacy as a priority. Your expense records, receipt photos, and personal financial data are stored locally on your device and are never sold or shared with third parties.

1. Information We Collect and Process

When you use Expense Tracker, the following information may be processed directly on your device:

  • Expense Records: Amounts, dates, categories, payment methods, and descriptions that you choose to enter.
  • Receipts & Images: Images of receipts or documents you capture using your device camera or select from your photo library.
  • Voice Input (Speech Data): When using voice input to record expenses, audio is converted to text using your device's native speech recognition framework.

2. Device Permissions Used

To provide core features, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: Allows you to capture photos of physical receipts to attach to expense items.
  • Photo Library / Storage: Allows you to select receipt images stored on your device and save/export generated PDF reports.
  • Microphone / Speech Recognition: Allows speech-to-text functionality for dictating expense details quickly. Audio data is processed locally or via system-level speech services and is not stored or shared on external servers by this app.
  • Network State: Used solely to check connectivity status when performing network-dependent operations.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data entered into Expense Tracker is stored locally on your device using encrypted/secure SQLite local storage and the device's sandboxed file system. We do not maintain external servers to collect, upload, or sync your personal expense entries without your explicit action.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information. When you use the app to generate reports (such as PDF summaries) or share receipt files, data is only transmitted through system sharing interfaces that you explicitly initiate and control.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete individual expense records, attachments, or categories at any time from within the application.
  • Clearing application data from your device settings or uninstalling the app will permanently remove all stored local databases and associated receipt images.
  • To request deletion of any support correspondence or account records, you can submit an online request via our Account & Data Deletion Page.

6. Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
